# Outreach photos

These images were supplied by the team from three outreach archives (`outreachone.zip`,
`outreachtwo.zip`, `outreachthree.zip`) plus the portrait of the group leader.

- 28 unique outreach photos were kept. Exact duplicate files were removed.
- Files were resized to a maximum of 1200px, stripped of metadata and re-saved as
  progressive JPEGs for the web. Original filenames are mapped below.
- `leader-shanis.jpg` is the supplied portrait of **Akatucungura Shanis Pretty**.

Captions on the website are intentionally neutral until the team confirms what each
photo shows. Before publishing, confirm that everyone pictured (or their guardian)
is happy for the image to appear online.

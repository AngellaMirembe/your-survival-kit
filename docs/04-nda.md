# One-Page Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Between:** [Your Full Name], developer ("Disclosing Party")
**And:** Your Survival Kit, represented by [Client Name] ("Receiving Party")
**Date:** [Date]

## 1. Purpose

The parties are working together on the design and development of a website for Your Survival Kit. In that context, each party may share confidential information.

## 2. Confidential information

Includes, but is not limited to: client contact details, supporter and donor data, beneficiary information, photographs, internal plans, business documents, source code, database credentials and the website source code before public launch.

## 3. Obligations

The Receiving Party shall:
- use the information only for the website project;
- not disclose it to third parties without written permission;
- protect it with reasonable care and report any suspected breach immediately;
- not publish personal data or images of children without written guardian consent.

## 4. Exclusions

Information that is already public, independently developed, or required by law to be disclosed is not confidential.

## 5. Term

This agreement applies from the date above and continues for **two (2) years** after the project ends.

## 6. Return / destruction

On request, each party will return or securely destroy confidential material, except for one archival copy required by law or professional practice.

Why we exist

Small gifts, gathered together, become real change.

Too many children start the day without the basics that make school and home feel safe. A notebook, a warm layer, a bar of soap — these are ordinary to us and life-changing to them.

Your Survival Kit removes the distance between wanting to help and actually helping. We gather what our community already has, sort it with care, and deliver it with dignity to children in unprivileged situations.

Fast & local

We respond within our own communities.

Transparent

Every gift is recorded and accounted for.

What we do

Four ways we show up

Practical support designed around what children actually need.

School Survival Kits

Notebooks, pencils, bags and basic stationery so every child can learn with confidence.

Clothing & Warmth

Sweaters, socks, blankets and raincoats collected from families and passed on with care.

Food & Nutrition

Non-perishable snacks and reusable bottles for children who arrive at school hungry.

Mentorship & Play

Young volunteers who listen, play and remind children that they are not forgotten.

Simple by design

How the kit reaches a child

Three steps, no middle layers, no unnecessary overhead.

Step 1: Gather

Families, schools and young people contribute items they no longer need.

Step 2: Pack & sort

Volunteers check quality, group items by age and need, and add a note.

Step 3: Deliver

We hand kits directly to families and schools, then record the delivery.

Get involved

There is room for you here.

You do not need money to make a difference. Time, attention and a little organisation go a long way.

Give your time

Help sort donations, pack kits or visit a partner school. We train you in a single afternoon.

Partner with us

Schools, businesses and churches can host a collection drive or sponsor a programme.

Collect items

Run a drive in your class, host or neighbourhood. We give you a clear list and a collection log.

Spread the word

Share our stories, bring a friend, or write about what you notice in your own community.

What happens after you sign up?

  1. 1We read your application and match your interests to an active team.
  2. 2A volunteer lead calls or emails you to say hello and answer questions.
  3. 3You join a short induction — no experience needed.
  4. 4You show up, help a child, and tell us what you learned.

Our programmes

Practical help, delivered with dignity.

We do a few things well. Each programme is built around something a child can actually use, wear, eat or learn from.

The model

Gather. Pack. Deliver.

1. Gather

Families donate items they no longer need; others contribute funds and we buy deliberately.

2. Pack

Volunteers check quality, match items to age and climate, and add a handwritten note.

3. Deliver

We hand kits to schools and families directly, confirm receipt and share an update with donors.

School Survival Kits

A backpack with notebooks, pencils, an exercise book, a ruler and a reusable bottle. Built for a child starting school with confidence.

  • Backpack or sturdy bag
  • Notebooks & pencils
  • Eraser, ruler, sharpener
  • Reusable water bottle
